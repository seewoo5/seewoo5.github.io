About

The Sphere-Packing-Lean project formalizes the solution of the sphere packing problem in dimension 8 in Lean. Its Packathons are usually weekly online meetings. This one is in person: five days at the Bernoulli Center at EPFL, working on the formalization together.

The aim is to push the project forward: this is a working week, not a Lean tutorial. We expect most participants to have some formalization experience, but newcomers are welcome too. Most of each day is spent in working groups, listed on the Groups page. The week opens with introductory lectures on Monday and Tuesday. Some sessions will be hybrid, so people who can’t travel to Lausanne can join remotely.

Participation is primarily in person, by application until 30 October 2026, 23:59 CET. Accommodation is funded for all selected participants.

See the Lean Zulip announcement and discussion thread for more details.
