Getting there

Lausanne is about 45 minutes by direct train from Geneva Airport.

From Lausanne station, take the M2 metro one stop to Lausanne-Flon, then the M1 towards Renens-Gare and get off at UNIL-Sorge, about 15 minutes from Flon. The Bernoulli Center is a five-minute walk from the stop.
